# India Market Context

- Timezone: Asia/Kolkata.
- Regular NSE cash market: 09:15 to 15:30 IST on non-holiday weekdays.
- TradeLoop executable scope: Indian cash equities only.
- Indices are context/benchmark inputs only.
- Products allowed: CNC and MIS.
- Product forbidden: NRML.
- Segments forbidden: futures, options, commodities, currencies, SME.
- Direction: long-only. `SELL` is allowed only as an exit from an existing long.
