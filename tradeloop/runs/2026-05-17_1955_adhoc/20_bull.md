# Bull Researcher

## RELIANCE Bull Case

1. RIL is still a high-liquidity, diversified large-cap with multiple earnings
   engines. Official management commentary cited FY26 revenue growth around 10%
   and EBITDA growth around 13.5%, with consumer businesses contributing more
   than 55% of EBITDA. Evidence: `10_news.md`.

2. Jio remains a credible medium-term catalyst. Management cited 524 million
   subscribers, strong 5G/broadband growth, and FY26 Jio revenue around
   Rs 1,46,885 crore. The company also continues to frame Jio Platforms listing
   progress as an important milestone. Evidence: `10_news.md`.

3. Balance-sheet and cash-flow metrics do not show a fundamental veto.
   StockAnalysis shows debt/equity 0.37, net debt/EBITDA 0.86, operating cash
   flow Rs 1.92T, and free cash flow Rs 691.97B. Evidence:
   `12_fundamentals.md`.

4. The share has already corrected sharply. A 10-day decline of about 8.66% and
   weak broad-market tone may create a swing opportunity if price stabilizes and
   reclaims 1365-1389. Evidence: `13_technical.md`.

5. Liquidity is excellent for a paper swing setup. Average 20-day volume is
   about 20.0 million shares, so TradeLoop's small position sizes would not
   create liquidity risk. Evidence: `13_technical.md`.

## Bull Conclusion

The bull case supports keeping RELIANCE on watch. It does not support buying
before technical repair because the catalyst is medium-term and the current
price action is still weak.
