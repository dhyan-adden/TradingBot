## Stocks With Significant Corporate Announcements

### RELIANCE

- RIL published official financial and operational performance for the quarter
  and year ended Mar 31, 2026. The audited results were approved by the Board
  and Audit Committee, and auditors issued an unmodified opinion. Source:
  `01_news_raw.md`.
- Management said FY26 revenue grew about 10% and EBITDA about 13.5%; consumer
  businesses contributed more than 55% of EBITDA. This supports the diversified
  franchise thesis. Source: `01_news_raw.md`.
- Jio remains the cleanest positive segment: management cited strong subscriber
  additions, 524 million subscribers, and FY26 Jio revenue of about
  Rs 1,46,885 crore. Jio listing progress remains a medium-term optionality
  catalyst, not a precise near-term entry trigger. Source: `01_news_raw.md`.
- O2C and macro are the main offsets. Management explicitly discussed West Asia
  supply shock, rupee depreciation, physical crude limitations, logistics and
  insurance costs, and under-recoveries. Source: `01_news_raw.md`.

## Macro Themes Worth Weighting Today

- Rupee weakness can pressure imported inflation and energy margins.
- A Nifty-heavyweight long setup needs index stabilization; broad weekly market
  weakness reduces the quality of buying a falling large-cap before confirmation.
