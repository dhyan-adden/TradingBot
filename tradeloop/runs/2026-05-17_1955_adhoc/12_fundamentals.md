  subscribers and FY26 Jio revenue around Rs 1,46,885 crore. Source:
  `10_news.md`.

  https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/RELIANCE/statistics/

Risks:

- Q4 commentary and financial-media coverage show pressure from West Asia,
  crude, freight/insurance costs, and weaker O2C conditions. Source:
  `10_news.md`.
- Return profile is adequate but not exceptional for a fresh swing by itself:
  StockAnalysis shows ROE 9.14%, ROIC 7.71%, and dividend yield 0.45%. Source:
  https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/RELIANCE/statistics/
- Valuation is not distressed enough to ignore technical timing. A large-cap
  long should still require trend repair or reversal evidence.
