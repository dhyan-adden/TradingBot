## RELIANCE Debate

### Round 1
Bull: RELIANCE has strong FY26 scale, consumer businesses are cushioning energy cyclicality, Jio listing optionality can improve sentiment, and the share price has already corrected.

Bear: The request is for a 5-20 day CNC swing setup, not a long-term franchise call. The latest chart does not show entry confirmation: close is below short averages, RSI is weak, MACD has rolled negative, and recent selling pressure is unresolved.

### Round 2
Bull: A bounce from near Rs 1,300 support could offer favorable upside toward Rs 1,388-1,405 and then higher if the broader market stabilizes.

Bear: A bounce setup needs evidence of reversal or reclaim. Buying before confirmation makes the trade depend on support holding while the dominant recent move is down. The first target zone overlaps with broken moving-average resistance, limiting reward quality.

### Round 3
Bull: Fundamentals are not red; promoter pledge is not an issue, leverage is manageable, and the company remains one of India's highest-quality liquid large caps.

Bear: Fundamentals being "not red" is not the same as a justified trade. With no positive strategy-performance memory and no clean technical trigger, the correct long-only action is watch/pass rather than forcing a paper order.

Ticker: RELIANCE
