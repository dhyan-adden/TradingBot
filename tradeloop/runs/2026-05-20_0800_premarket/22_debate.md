# Debate Moderator

## Round 1

Bull: INFY is the cleanest candidate because it combines sector leadership,
rupee/exporter tailwind, and a close near the 19 May high.

Bear: The setup still lacks project OHLCV, ATR, current premarket quote, and a
hard stop. One strong session can reverse at the open, especially in a volatile
macro tape.

## Round 2

Bull: TCS and SBIN add alternative large-cap watch candidates if INFY gaps too
far or if rotation broadens.

Bear: TCS remains below major moving averages from public context, and SBIN is
inside a stressed financial sector. Neither has enough confirmation to become
the trade instead of INFY.

## Round 3

Bull: Reliance is close to prior support and remains a high-quality watchlist
franchise.

Bear: Reliance is explicitly below its carry-forward reclaim zone, and the
current macro mix hurts the O2C-linked thesis. That is a pass, not a buy.

Trader stage.
