## HDFCBANK — Bear Case

*Technical classification from 13_technical.md: Bullish continuation / breakout-watch. Only name advancing from this run.*

### 1. RSI 68.5 — momentum buffer is nearly exhausted

RSI at 68.5 is the highest reading in the four-name universe and is already inside overbought territory. For a swing entry targeting 5-20 days, entering with RSI at 68-70 compresses the upside window. Historically, RSI pushing through 70 in a choppy index environment often precedes a mean-reversion selloff, not continuation. The bull must immediately absorb an RSI headwind.

### 2. Trend classification is "chop," not confirmed uptrend

13_technical.md acknowledges the trend label is "chop (transitioning bullish)" — not a clean uptrend. The 60D return of +1.8% over two months is barely above zero; this is not a stock with demonstrated positive momentum over the medium term. NIFTY itself had a mild down day (closed 24056, intraday high 24261). Entering a chop stock on a down-index day with RSI overbought is a low-edge environment for a swing long.

### 3. Hard resistance at the 20-day high (799) has not yet been cleared

Price closed at 796.30, which is 0.3% below the 20-day high of 799. The entry thesis requires a confirmed breakout above 799.50, but as of yesterday's close that breakout has NOT occurred. Entering before the trigger fires violates breakout discipline. If the index opens weak today, price may fade back below the SMA20 cluster (765-773) before any breakout happens.

### 4. No news catalyst in this run

01_news_raw.md is explicitly empty. The only engine driving an HDFCBANK long is price-relative-strength. That is a valid setup class, but it is also the weakest form of evidence — no earnings beat, no credit-quality improvement, no RBI rate narrative, no FII flow data. The risk-reward asymmetry without a catalyst is worse than when a catalyst exists.

### 5. Mildly negative tape is a near-term headwind

NIFTY fell from intraday high 24261 to close 24056 — a 205-point fade on the day's range. A weak opening today could act as a gravity pull even on the strongest relative-strength stock. The RSI at 68.5 leaves little cushion if sector rotation or large-cap selling resumes.

### 6. Stop placement risk (775) is 2.7% below current price

The technical analysis proposes a stop at 775 (below SMA20 and SMA50 cluster). From close 796.30, that is a 21.3-point (2.67%) risk per share. On a Rs. 100,000 account with 1.5% risk/trade (Rs. 1,500 at risk), position size would be approximately 70 shares (Rs. 1,500 / Rs. 21.30). At 70 shares x Rs. 796 = Rs. 55,720, that is a 55.7% position-weight — more than double the 25% per-position cap. The stop placement as stated is policy-incompatible; narrowing the stop to fit the policy constraint would push it into the consolidation zone where noise stops are likely.

**Bear verdict for HDFCBANK:** Legitimate setup with real relative strength, but the entry is premature (breakout not yet confirmed), RSI headroom is thin, catalyst evidence is absent, and the natural stop placement violates position-size policy. A pass on day-one entry is justified; revisit only if price confirms a clean hold above 799.50 with a policy-compliant stop structure.

## INFY — Bear Case

*Technical classification from 13_technical.md: Avoid (falling knife; oversold does not equal buy). No long setup proposed.*

### 1. Weakest name in universe by every measurable metric

INFY is -16.3% from its 20-day high, -16.8% on the 60D, and -7.7% over just 5 days. This is not a stock pulling back — it is a stock in freefall. The rate of decline is accelerating, not decelerating.

### 2. RSI 28.2 — deeply oversold in a downtrend is the strongest bear continuation signal

The most dangerous mistake in swing trading is treating RSI oversold readings in downtrends as buy signals. RSI 28 in a confirmed downtrend (price 90 points below SMA20, SMA20 below SMA50) means sellers are overwhelmingly in control and there is no bottom yet defined. The oversold condition can persist for weeks or deepen further (to RSI 15-20) before any reversal occurs.

### 3. No base, no stabilisation, no reversal structure

A valid long setup after a downtrend requires at minimum: multi-day sideways base above a key support, decreasing volume on down days, and a reclaim of a short-term moving average. None of these conditions are visible in the available data. INFY has no floor in the current indicator set.

### 4. Entry risk is unquantifiable

Without a defined support level or base to anchor a stop, a long entry today would require either a very wide stop (accepting large per-share risk) or a very tight stop (virtually certain to be hit by normal intraday volatility on a falling-knife name). Neither option produces a valid risk/reward trade structure.

### 5. "Catching a falling knife" — pump-risk flag

Any short-term bounce in INFY from RSI-28 oversold should be treated with extreme caution. In large-cap Indian IT, institutional holders who have been holding through a -16.8% decline will use any bounce as an exit opportunity (supply overhang). A retail swing long entering on oversold optimism faces heavy supply from institutional exits at every recovery level.

**Bear verdict for INFY:** Hard pass. This is a falling knife setup. No capital should be deployed. Revisit only after a multi-day base above SMA20 (1130) with RSI recovering through 40.
