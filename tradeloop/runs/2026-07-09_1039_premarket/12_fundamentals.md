Gross NPA stable sub-1.5%; PCR healthy.
No concentrated promoter pledge (institutional-held structure).

**India-specific red flags check:**
- Promoter pledge >25%: Not applicable - no promoter pledge concern.
- Auditor resignation: None on record.
- Related-party transactions: None flagged at material/reportable threshold.
- Negative operating cash flow (repeated): Not applicable - banking business; NII and fee income consistently positive.

**News overlay:** Today's ED freeze on Trinamool-linked accounts is political noise with zero capital or regulatory implication for the bank itself (Tier-C only, no Tier-A corroboration).
MCLR revision is a routine operational event.
ICICI Securities BUY reiteration at Rs 1850 target is mildly supportive.
Macro rate-hike read (Iran/Fed) is a sector-level headwind shared across all banks - not HDFCBANK-specific.

**Verdict: GREEN.** No fundamental reason to exit or avoid holding. Position within thesis.

### SBIN - YELLOW (current holding)

**Fundamental profile:** Largest PSU bank by assets; strong franchise with government ownership backstop.
PSU bank fundamentals carry structural caveats: government-mandate lending (priority sector, MSME, Kisan credit) carries elevated NPA risk vs private peers.
ROE has structurally lagged HDFCBANK over multi-year periods.
Government of India owns ~57%; no promoter pledge.

**India-specific red flags check:**
- Promoter pledge >25%: None - GoI holds directly.
- Auditor resignation: None on record.
- Related-party transactions: Government-directed lending is disclosed; no unexplained RPT.
- Negative operating cash flow (repeated): Not applicable - banking; PPOP margins have periodically compressed on wage revision cycles but not a recurring FCF concern.

**News overlay:** Jefferies structural positives (13% loan CAGR, 14% ROE) are medium-term; not a near-term price catalyst.
Bank Nifty laggard framing (Tier-C) is consistent with historical PSU-vs-private rotation.
G-Sec portfolio duration exposure to any domestic bond yield spike (sympathetic to Japan 10Y / Fed hike narrative) is the key macro risk for SBIN specifically today - MTM pressure on AFS book if domestic yields move up.

**Verdict: YELLOW.** Fundamentals are acceptable but PSU structural discount is real, and today's global bond yield spike narrative creates a specific near-term G-Sec MTM headwind that HDFCBANK faces less acutely.
Hold within hard stop (Rs 1015.40); do not add today.
